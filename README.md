# The World Today — Sunday 22 November 2026

A newspaper front page in one image, summing up the world news of Sunday
22 November 2026: Iran's tanker strikes and the Houthi attack on the Saudi
capital as the lead story, with the day's other headlines from Washington,
the markets, the Americas, Europe and Asia, and a dateline globe marking
where each story broke.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 22 November's world news in one
image" bounty ($100 in USDC): https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122

- `render_poster.py` draws `poster.png` (1024×1024) with Pillow.
- `main.py` is the entry program: it copies `poster.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
